# 🛵 Rapido Bike Taxi Brand Analysis

### 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Rapido's business and brand performance using data-driven analysis.

The objective is to understand business growth, customer trends, market performance, and key factors influencing the brand.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze brand performance
- Understand customer trends
- Evaluate business growth
- Analyze market positioning
- Identify growth opportunities
- Generate data-driven recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Charts
- Data Analysis
- Business Analytics

---

## 📊 Analysis Areas

- Revenue Performance
- Customer Trends
- Market Performance
- Brand Growth
- Pricing
- Customer Experience

---

## 📈 Dashboard

The Excel dashboard provides interactive analysis using:

- Pivot Tables
- Charts
- KPI Cards
- Filters
- Business Metrics

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Analyzed Rapido's business growth.
- Identified important customer and market trends.
- Evaluated factors influencing brand performance.
- Identified potential growth opportunities.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Strengthen high-performing market segments.
- Improve customer experience.
- Monitor pricing and demand.
- Use customer data to optimize marketing.
- Focus on sustainable business growth.
